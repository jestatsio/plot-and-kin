# Fixture provenance

Behavioral examples may be synthetic. Label invented people, addresses, claims, and source text prominently. Synthetic examples test evidence handling and are not historical findings.

Sampled DC connector responses and any archival document-reading examples are maintained with their provenance in `tests/fixtures/`. A non-DC manuscript or NARA example is unrelated to the DC property sample unless separate evidence establishes a relationship. Never combine them into one purported case history.

Fixtures show the captured response or document, not current upstream availability, complete record coverage, or permission to redistribute every underlying source.
